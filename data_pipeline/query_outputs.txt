Query 6 - JOIN
============================================================

SQL:
SELECT
    b.title,
    c.category_name,
    b.price_gbp,
    b.rating,
    b.in_stock
FROM books AS b
JOIN categories AS c
    ON b.category_id = c.category_id
ORDER BY b.rating DESC, b.price_gbp DESC
LIMIT 10;
